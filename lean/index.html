Privacy Policy for איך מגיעים · Eich Magiim

Effective date: 2026-09-12
Apps: Eich Magiim for iPhone and Apple Watch (me.nav-app), Eich Magiim for Android (me.navapp) and the Eich Magiim web app
Contact: shrnshlm@gmail.com

Eich Magiim plans trips in Israel by bus and train, on foot, and by shared scooter. It does not require an account, does not show ads and does not track you.

What stays on your device

  • The Home and Work places you save, your recent searches and your language choice.

These are stored only on your device (or, for the web app, in your browser) and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data, removes them.

What is sent to our server

To plan a trip, the app sends requests to our own server, which we run at Hetzner Online. Each request contains only what is needed to answer it:

  • Trip requests — the start and end coordinates of the trip (your current location, if you plan from where you are), the date and time, and whether you want to depart or arrive by that time.
  • Place search (Android and web) — the text you type, and your approximate position so that nearby places are listed first. On iPhone and Apple Watch, place search is handled by Apple Maps instead and never reaches our server.
  • Scooters and zones — the area of the trip, so we can show nearby scooters and the areas where scooters may not ride or park.
  • Nearby information on Apple Watch and in widgets — your location, to show the closest scooters and transit.
  • An installation ID — the iPhone and Android apps create a random ID when first opened and send it with trip requests, so we can count how many installations use the service. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or advertising ID, and reinstalling the app creates a new one. The web app and the watch do not send it.

Like any web server, ours logs the IP address, time and address of each request for operation and security. We keep these logs for a limited period, do not use them to build profiles, and do not sell or share them.

To answer a place search from Android or the web, our server forwards the search text (without your IP address) to the Photon geocoder run by komoot. Transit timetables and live data come from Israel's Ministry of Transport; scooter data comes from the operators' public feeds. None of these receive information about you.

What your device sends directly to third parties

  • OpenStreetMap tile servers (tile.openstreetmap.org) — when you view the map, your device requests map images for the area shown, which reveals your IP address and that area. See the OSMF Privacy Policy.
  • Apple Maps (iPhone and Apple Watch) — place search uses Apple's MapKit, which sends the text you type and your approximate location to Apple. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • unpkg.com — the iPhone app and the web app load the open-source Leaflet map library from this public CDN, which receives your IP address.
  • Scooter operators — if you tap a scooter and choose to open it, the operator's app or website (Lime, Bird or Dott) opens, and their privacy policy applies from then on.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers or track you across apps and websites.
  • We do not sell or share data with data brokers.
  • We do not require sign-in or collect contact details.

Permissions

  • Location (while using the app) — used to plan trips from where you are and to show buses and scooters near you. If you deny it, you can still plan trips by typing a starting point.
  • Internet and network state — required to fetch routes, map images and scooter data.

Children

Eich Magiim is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Because Eich Magiim has no accounts, we hold no profile about you. Server logs are deleted on a rolling basis. For any question or request under the GDPR, the Israeli Privacy Protection Law or other laws, email us at the address above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above changes when we do, and material changes come with a new app release.
